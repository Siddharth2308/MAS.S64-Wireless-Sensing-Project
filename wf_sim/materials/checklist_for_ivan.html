Measured reflectivity (not in the CSV yet)

DCA1000 raw captures (E5), relative to a 6 mm aluminium plate at 40 cm, straight-on, 5 re-placements each. Thickness is nominal.

Thickness changes reflection as much as material does, so reflectivity should come from material and wall_mm together. Cardboard, wood, rubber and the rest need model estimates. Planned file: material_reflectivity.csv.

mmRender plan (staff asked for tasks, outcome, metrics)

Task
Give each point a reflectivity weight from these labels. mmRender currently weights all points equally.
Expected
Plastic and glass parts render weaker than metal, by up to about 15 dB in E5.
Metric
Rendered vs real (MITO) radar images of the same objects, before and after. Later: reconstruction recall and precision on the 24 multi-material objects.
